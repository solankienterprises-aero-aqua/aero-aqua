About Us

Trust in Every Drop

Aero Aqua, operated under Solanki Enterprises by Kamlesh Singh Solanki (Bhupendra Singh Rajput), is established with the vision of providing pure, clean, and mineral-rich drinking water at an affordable price to every citizen.
